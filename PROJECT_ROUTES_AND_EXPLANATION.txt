================================================================================
          ILA AI HUB & COURSE CREATOR ECOSYSTEM - ARCHITECTURE & ROUTES
================================================================================

This project is built as a high-performance Single Page Application (SPA) with a
state-driven navigation router in src/App.tsx, accompanied by specialized module
views and sub-views.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. PRIMARY NAVIGATION ROUTES (Top-Level Navigation Bar)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

1.1 'course_creator' (Flagship Course Creator Studio)
    - Path/State: primaryNavView = 'course_creator' | activeModule = 'course_creator'
    - Component: SearchBox + CourseWorkspaceView + QuestionTreeDrawer
    - Explanation: The primary educational curriculum authoring studio. Generates
      multi-chapter course outlines, book plans, student outcomes, lecture notes,
      and modular lesson plans using Google Gemini AI.

1.2 'tieup_creator' (Tie-up & Academic Partnership Creator)
    - Path/State: primaryNavView = 'tieup_creator' | activeModule = 'ai_tieup_creator'
    - Component: <TieupResearchEngineView />
    - Explanation: B2B university tie-up, articulation research, and institutional
      outreach system. Manages partner leads, draft MoUs, and automated email
      dispatch via EmailJS.

1.3 'library' (Dedicated Curriculum Library & Reader)
    - Path/State: primaryNavView = 'library'
    - Component: <DedicatedLibraryView />
    - Explanation: Full catalog of all authored courses, curriculum plans, and synced
      course books. Provides complete reading mode, course exports, and Supabase
      cloud sync.

1.4 'student_paths' (Delivery Paths & Learning Mode Hub)
    - Path/State: primaryNavView = 'student_paths'
    - Component: <LearningPathModeView />
    - Explanation: Specialized educational delivery modes for courses (1-on-1,
      group cohort, online camps, physical campus clinics, and athletic training).

1.5 'chat_home' (Universal AI Companion & Guide)
    - Path/State: primaryNavView = 'chat_home' | activeModule = 'ila_chat'
    - Component: <ChatHomeView />
    - Explanation: 24/7 conversational mentor and platform navigator. Answers student
      queries, compares international study destinations, and provides interactive AI guidance.

1.6 'resources' (Resources Data Hub)
    - Path/State: primaryNavView = 'resources'
    - Component: <ResourcesDataHubView />
    - Explanation: Centralized repository of all saved outputs, research files,
      transcripts, generated assets, and institutional lead datasets.

1.7 'central_dashboard' (Platform Analytics & Activity Dashboard)
    - Path/State: primaryNavView = 'central_dashboard'
    - Component: <CentralDashboardView />
    - Explanation: Analytics dashboard displaying session activity, course creation
      stats, tie-up conversion rates, database health, and quick actions.

1.8 'ai_tool' (Dedicated AI Hub Specialized Tool Workspace)
    - Path/State: primaryNavView = 'ai_tool'
    - Component: <AIHubWorkspaceView />
    - Explanation: Dedicated interactive workspace for any of the 17 specialized
      AI hub products selected from the AI Hub module dropdown.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
2. TIE-UP & PARTNERSHIP CREATOR SUB-ROUTES (TieupResearchEngineView.tsx)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

2.1 'chat_home' (Research & Institutional Discovery)
    - Tab: activeMainTab = 'chat_home'
    - Explanation: AI search and discovery engine for international universities,
      ranking filters, IELTS/German language requirements, and commission models.

2.2 'resources' (Institutional Leads Repository)
    - Tab: activeMainTab = 'resources'
    - Explanation: Filterable directory of university leads. Allows grouping, filtering
      by country/margin, and moving candidates into the active outreach pipeline.

2.3 'process' (Outreach Pipeline & EmailJS Dispatch Stream)
    - Tab: activeMainTab = 'process'
    - Explanation: Active outreach campaign manager. Allows selecting leads, previewing
      email scripts with dynamic variables, dispatching bulk outreach via EmailJS,
      and auditing delivery and bounce logs.

2.4 'partners' (Partner Portfolios & Articulation Agreements)
    - Tab: activeMainTab = 'partners'
    - Explanation: Confirmed partner institutions with finalized MoUs, articulation
      agreements, active revenue-sharing frameworks, and student pipelines.

2.5 'policies' (MoU Legal Terms & Partnership Policies)
    - Tab: activeMainTab = 'policies'
    - Explanation: Standardized bilateral agreement clauses, accreditation guidelines,
      commission tiers, and fee reconciliation policies.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
3. COURSE CREATOR SUB-STUDIOS & DELIVERY MODES (App.tsx)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

3.1 'home': Standard curriculum workspace with lesson syllabus outline generator.
3.2 'admin_library': Direct workspace access to saved course databases.
3.3 'slide_ai': Slide deck generator producing formatted teaching slides.
3.4 'video_ai': Masterclass video lecture studio and transcript generator.
3.5 'intelli_coach': Adaptive AI coach simulating live student interaction.
3.6 'one_on_one_online': Personalized 1-on-1 tutoring delivery configuration.
3.7 'group_online': Group cohort lecture and breakout delivery configuration.
3.8 'camp_online': Virtual intensive bootcamps and workshop paths.
3.9 'camp_offline': On-campus resident camps and laboratory workshops.
3.10 'sports_online': Online athletic mental conditioning and esports tactics.
3.11 'sports_offline': In-person physical drills and sports clinics.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
5. SYSTEM MODALS & OVERLAYS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

- Activity Tracker Modal: Real-time system execution and background task logs.
- AI Unified Parameter Modal: Model temperature, persona, and response depth tuner.
- Workspace Settings Dropdown: Theme selector (6 themes), contrast, font scaling.
- Teaching Slides Modal: Full-screen presentation mode for generated slides.
- Masterclass Video Player: Video lecture playback with interactive transcript.
